Read `memory.md` before starting any task: it holds the project's purpose, rules, open work and known workarounds.

Keep it current:
- Update `memory.md` right after a feature lands, a significant decision is made or a rule is set.
- Short bullets. Prune finished tasks and fold old detail into one line instead of letting it grow.
- Bump its "Last updated" line when you change it.
